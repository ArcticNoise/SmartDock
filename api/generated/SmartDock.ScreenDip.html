Place(double, double, double, double, double, double)

Window origin in DIPs. scaleX and scaleY are the window's DPI divided by 96 (1 at 100%, 1.25 at 125%, 1.5 at 150%). Grab offsets are DIPs from the window's top-left to the pointer, so the cursor stays on the spot that was grabbed. Device coordinates are negative on a monitor left of or above the primary.
