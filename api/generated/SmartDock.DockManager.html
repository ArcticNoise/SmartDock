Hosts a DockLayout. Register content by id, assign Layout, and the control builds the splits, tabs, floating windows, and auto-hide bars. Only the selected tab's content is in the tree. Drag a tab along its strip to reorder it, or off the strip to float it, then drop it on a guide to dock it. A tool, or a float of tools, can also drop on an auto-hide strip. Guides and the landing preview are drawn in a topmost overlay so the dragged window cannot cover them. Another float under the pointer shows that float's compass and edge guides; the topmost one wins, and the dragged window is skipped. A dock guide or that float wins where it overlaps an auto-hide hot zone. Documents are not an auto-hide target. The same gesture is covered by the internal drag methods, which call DockDragSession with coordinates and no mouse. Ctrl+Tab switches panes. DockCommands closes, cycles document tabs, and toggles float and auto-hide. When AnimationsEnabled is set and Windows animations are on, reordered tab headers slide and the dock guides fade. The auto-hide flyout is a child window so it paints above an HwndHost pane. It opens and closes at its arranged size: a render transform does not move that HWND. Those clocks do not change layout, focus, or events. ResizeGripThickness is the hit zone of a splitter, a flyout edge, and a float's border. ResizeMode applies splitter, flyout, and floating-window sizes live, once per frame, or as a preview on release.

Hit thickness, in device-independent pixels, of a splitter, a flyout resize edge, and a floating window's edges and corners. The default is 6. A smaller or non-finite value is raised to 6. The splitter stays a panel-colored gap and the flyout and float keep their 1px border. The float insets its client by this thickness, filled with the chrome color, so a native child cannot cover the edge. A maximized float has no band. This is not part of the layout JSON.

Methods

ActivatePane(string)

Brings a pane to the user and moves keyboard focus into its content when that content is focusable. A hidden pane is shown first, through ShowPane(string). A docked or floating tab is selected. A floating window is activated, and restored when it is minimized. An auto-hidden pane opens its flyout. The pane becomes ActiveContentId.
