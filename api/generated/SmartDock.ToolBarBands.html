HitSites(ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarSite>, string, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarBarRect>, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarBandRect>, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarTrayRect>, double, double)

Picks the topmost window under the pointer and the pane under it in that window, then a drop in this order: the outer TargetBand of the window, a band of the window, a band of the pane, a tray of the window, a tray of the pane, the window's EdgeSlop, then the pane's edge. With no pane toolbars this is Hit(DockRect, string, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarBarRect>, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarBandRect>, ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarTrayRect>, double, double, string, string) on the window, so the main window docks as before. A pane edge that lies within EdgeSlop of the window edge catches the next EdgeSlop inward, so it stays reachable. Anything else floats. No allocations.

Pack(ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarPackItem>, double, Span<double>)

Positions one band along its axis. positions[i] is the start of items[i]. Start-aligned items pack from 0 in index order, index 0 nearest the start, and Offset is the gap before each one. End-aligned items pack from bandLength toward the start, index 0 nearest the far edge, and the offset is the gap after the far edge or the outer neighbour. A non-positive or non-finite length packs the end-aligned items immediately after the start-aligned ones. When the band is too narrow, every end-aligned item shifts so the innermost one starts where the start-aligned items end. End-aligned items may extend past the band. They do not overlap start-aligned items.

public static void Pack(ReadOnlySpan<ToolBarPackItem> items, double bandLength, Span<double> positions)

Place(List<ToolBarEntry>, string, ToolBarDrop, double, double, double, double)

Writes drop onto the toolbar and reindexes that band. A float keeps the docked edge, band, index, offset, and alignment for DockBack(List<ToolBarEntry>, string). An end-aligned toolbar dropped on the same band after its start-aligned toolbars keeps that alignment and its offset, so it stays packed against the far edge. Dropped before one of them, onto a new band, or onto another edge, it packs from the start at the drop index. Index counts the band's toolbars in screen order, start-aligned ones first. End-aligned toolbars keep their indexes when a start-aligned one is placed.
